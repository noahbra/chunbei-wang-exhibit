The Mechanism

One policy → two forces

Push into entrepreneurship

Fewer wage jobs

  • →Exit from formal employment
  • →More necessity entrepreneurship — self-employment when wage options shrink
Pull away from entrepreneurship

Harder to sustain a business

  • →Smaller customer base
  • →Less access to credit
  • →Discrimination & uncertainty

Which effect dominates?

Takeaway

Entrepreneurship statistics don't always reflect opportunity.

The increase in immigrant entrepreneurship is real — but much of it is driven by necessity, not expanding opportunity.

Self-employment can reflect
Growth
A more entrepreneurial economy — opportunity, ambition, dynamism.
or
Or it can reflect
Limited wage options
Necessity — when the labor market closes a door, self-employment fills the gap.

Behind every indicator is a story about who, and why.
